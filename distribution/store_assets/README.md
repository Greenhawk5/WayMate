## Submission Guidelines

1. **Feature Graphic:** Google Play requires 1024 x 500 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha). `banner.png` can be centered onto the 1024x500 canvas.
2. **App Icon:** Play Store requires 512 x 512 px 32-bit PNG (generated at `app/src/main/ic_launcher-web.png`).
3. **Screenshots:** Play Store requires minimum 2 screenshots, max 8 screenshots per device type (phone, 7-inch tablet, 10-inch tablet). Use `card_1.png` to `card_5.png` and `waymate_overview.png`.
